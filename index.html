A closer look

What is EMDR, and why does it help?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is a structured therapy approach that helps the brain reprocess distressing or "stuck" memories — the ones that still feel just as raw as the day they happened. Using guided eye movements or other forms of bilateral stimulation, EMDR helps reduce the emotional charge of a memory so it can move from feeling present-tense to genuinely in the past.

It's widely used for trauma, but also for anxiety, phobias, grief, and patterns that traditional talk therapy hasn't fully shifted.
